Frequently Asked Questions
What is AgroMath?

AgroMath is a marketplace that connects farmers, bulk buyers and transporters. It helps them find each other, organise orders and coordinate delivery.

Who can use AgroMath?

Farmers who want to sell produce, buyers who want to buy in bulk, and transporters who want to handle delivery jobs.

How do I create an account?

Open AgroMath, enter your phone number and follow the registration steps. You may receive a one-time code to confirm that the phone number belongs to you.

What is the one-time code for?

It helps protect your account and confirm your phone number. Never give your code to another person. AgroMath does not use these codes for advertising.

How do I buy farm produce?

Register as a buyer, browse available produce, choose what you need and place an order. Follow the instructions shown on the order page.

How do I sell my produce?

Register as a farmer, add the produce you have for sale, including the quantity and other important details, then respond to buyer orders.

How does transport work?

When transport is needed, delivery can be coordinated with transporters. Pickup location, destination, timing and transport cost should be clear before the goods move.

Can pickup or delivery details change?

Yes, where the order allows it, but everyone involved should agree to the new information so there is no confusion.

What if there is a problem with an order?

Keep your order reference and any useful evidence, such as photos or messages. Contact AgroMath support as soon as possible and explain the problem clearly.
