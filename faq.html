Frequently Asked Questions
What is AgroMath?

AgroMath is a marketplace that connects approved sellers, buyers and transporters. It helps people trade foodstuff and agricultural products, organise orders, coordinate pickup or delivery and follow fulfilment steps.

Who can sell on AgroMath?

On AgroMath, the Farmer account is the current selling account. It may be used by approved farmers, foodstuff merchants, aggregators, wholesalers and other permitted agricultural-product sellers.

Do I need to be a farmer to sell?

No. You do not need to personally grow the products, but you must be approved to sell permitted foodstuff or agricultural products and provide accurate product and pickup information.

How do I sell on AgroMath?

Use the Farmer selling account, add the approved products you have for sale, include quantity and pickup details, then respond when buyers place order requests.

Can a farmer/seller or transporter also buy?

Yes. Sellers, buyers and transporters can all browse and buy products in the AgroMath marketplace.

How does delivery or pickup work?

The person placing the order chooses a saved delivery location or pickup arrangement. AgroMath keeps the relevant pickup and delivery details with the order for clear coordination.

How do transporter quotes work?

Where transporter delivery is required, eligible transporters can see delivery opportunities and submit a transport price. The buyer can then choose an available option in AgroMath.

How does payment work?

The buyer pays the locked order total through AgroMath's supported payment flow. The app shows payment status so participants can follow the next step.

What happens after delivery?

Delivery or collection moves through fulfilment stages. The buyer accepts the goods or reports an issue, then settlement proceeds where applicable.

How do I report an issue?

Keep your order reference and useful evidence such as photos or messages, then contact AgroMath support quickly and explain the problem clearly.
