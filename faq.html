AgroMath FAQ

Plain answers about buying and selling on AgroMath.

What does AgroMath do?

AgroMath connects farmers, food traders, businesses and buyers across Nigeria. Product availability, pickup and delivery options depend on the seller, location and order. AgroMath does not own inventory, is not a supermarket, and is not a logistics company.

Who can sell?

The seller account is for farmers and foodstuff traders. A seller can also be a small food business. Follow the setup and product review steps shown in your account.

How do I buy?

Open AgroMath, browse available listings and follow the ordering steps shown in your account. Review the available pickup or delivery arrangement and final total before paying.

How does pickup or seller delivery work?

You can collect the goods yourself, or the seller can bring them. Check the goods at handover so the order can move to the correct next step.

How does payment work?

Follow the payment instructions shown for your order and review the final total before paying. Contact support if you need help with a payment.
