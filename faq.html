AgroMath Help Guide

A simple pilot guide for creating an account, browsing products, buying, selling, transport, delivery or pickup, payment and support.

AccountBrowseCart and checkoutDelivery or pickupPaymentSellTransportAccept or reportGet help
1

Create an account and log in

Use your phone number to register, complete verification where shown, then log in with your PIN.

  1. Open AgroMath and choose the account path that matches what you want to do.
  2. Enter your phone number and complete OTP or account verification if AgroMath asks for it.
  3. Set or enter your PIN to access your account.
2

Browse products

Open the marketplace, select a product and check the information before adding anything to cart.

  1. Open the marketplace from the AgroMath app.
  2. Choose a product card to view more details.
  3. Check price, unit, seller, available quantity and pickup or delivery information before continuing.
3

Add a product to cart and checkout

Choose the quantity you need, add the product to cart, then review the cart before checkout.

  1. Select the quantity using the controls shown on the product screen.
  2. Add the product to cart.
  3. Open the cart, review product, quantity, unit price and total, then continue to checkout.
4

Choose delivery or pickup

The person placing the order chooses a saved delivery location, buyer pickup, or a transporter quote option where available.

  1. Select a saved delivery location if the order should be delivered.
  2. Choose buyer pickup if you will collect the goods yourself.
  3. Use transporter quotes where the order requires delivery support from a transporter.
5

Make payment

The buyer pays the locked total using the supported AgroMath payment flow shown in the pilot app.

  1. Review the locked order total before payment.
  2. Use the payment flow currently enabled in the pilot app.
  3. Follow the payment status shown in AgroMath before fulfilment moves ahead.
Pilot note: This guide does not imply live collection when a pilot configuration has collection disabled. Follow the payment options shown inside AgroMath for the current pilot.
6

Sell on AgroMath

The current Farmer account is the selling account for approved farmers and other permitted sellers.

  1. Use the Farmer account if you are an approved seller.
  2. Add product details, quantity, price, unit and pickup information.
  3. Submit listings through the approval flow shown in AgroMath before buyers can order where approval is required.
Role wording: Do not rename the backend role. In the current app, Farmer means the selling account. Approved farmers, foodstuff merchants, aggregators, wholesalers and other permitted foodstuff or agricultural-product sellers may use it.
7

Transport with AgroMath

Transporters can find delivery jobs, quote, receive assignments and update delivery progress.

  1. Open available delivery jobs that match your route and capacity.
  2. Submit a transport quote where quoting is available.
  3. When assigned, start delivery and mark the order delivered after fulfilment.
8

Accept delivery or report an issue

After delivery or pickup, the buyer accepts the goods or reports a problem so the order can move to the correct next step.

  1. Check the delivered or collected goods against the order.
  2. Accept fulfilment if everything is correct.
  3. Report an issue quickly if the order is wrong, incomplete or damaged.
Settlement note: Settlement proceeds after the correct fulfilment and acceptance steps, or after issue handling where applicable.
9
