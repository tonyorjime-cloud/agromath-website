Common questions

Quick answers before you get started.

We have kept the explanations short and practical.

What exactly does AgroMath do?

AgroMath brings farmers, bulk buyers and transporters into one marketplace so they can find each other, organise orders and coordinate delivery.

How do I buy farm produce?

Open AgroMath, register as a buyer, browse available produce, choose the quantity you want and follow the order steps shown in the app.

How do I sell my produce?

Register as a farmer, add the produce you have for sale and respond when buyers place order requests.

How will my goods be delivered?

Where transport is required, delivery can be coordinated with transporters through the platform. Pickup, destination and transport cost should be agreed before movement.
