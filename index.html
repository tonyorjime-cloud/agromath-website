Common questions

Quick answers before you get started.

Plain answers about selling, buying, transport, payment and support.

What exactly does AgroMath do?

AgroMath connects approved sellers, buyers and transporters so they can trade foodstuff and agricultural products, organise orders and coordinate pickup or delivery.

Who can sell on AgroMath?

The Farmer account is the current selling account. Approved farmers, foodstuff merchants, aggregators, wholesalers and other permitted agricultural-product sellers may use it.

How do I buy products?

Open AgroMath, browse available products, choose what you need and follow the order steps shown in the app.

How will my order be delivered?

Where transport is required, delivery can be coordinated with transporters through AgroMath. Pickup, destination and transport cost should be clear before movement.
