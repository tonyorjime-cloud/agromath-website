Service Description

AgroMath is a marketplace that helps approved sellers, buyers and transporters coordinate trade in foodstuff and agricultural products. Participants may browse and buy from the marketplace, while selling and transporter tools support listing, quoting and fulfilment workflows where available.

Role Definitions

  • Seller / Farmer account: The AgroMath Farmer account is the current selling account. It may be used by approved farmers, foodstuff merchants, aggregators, wholesalers and other permitted sellers of foodstuff and agricultural products.
  • Buyer: A person or business placing an order for products through AgroMath.
  • Transporter: A person or business offering transport services for eligible delivery opportunities.

Account, PIN and OTP Use

  • Users must provide a valid phone number.
  • PIN or OTP steps may be required for login, verification and account recovery.
  • Verification messages are user-initiated or service-triggered only.
  • Sharing PIN or OTP codes is prohibited.

Acceptable Use

  • No spam, phishing or unsolicited messaging.
  • No illegal, prohibited or misleading product listings.
  • No attempt to bypass telecom, payment, security or platform rules.

Marketplace and Fulfilment Responsibilities

  • Seller/Farmer-account users: Provide accurate product, quantity, price and pickup information for permitted products.
  • Buyers: Provide accurate order and delivery information, including saved delivery locations where used, and follow payment and acceptance steps shown in AgroMath.
  • Transporters: Quote honestly and deliver in good faith when selected for delivery work.
  • All participants: Follow order, delivery or pickup, payment, fulfilment, acceptance and issue-reporting steps shown in AgroMath.

Payment and Settlement

Where supported, payment proceeds through AgroMath's payment workflow. Delivery or pickup may move through fulfilment milestones, and buyer acceptance helps accepted fulfilment proceed toward settlement. AgroMath does not use this website to promise guaranteed delivery, guaranteed product quality, guaranteed refunds, instant settlement or a specific dispute outcome.

Location and Logistics Information

AgroMath may use saved delivery locations, pickup information, distance calculations and fulfilment status to coordinate orders. Location information is for order and delivery coordination and should not be treated as continuous live vehicle tracking unless AgroMath explicitly provides that feature for a specific workflow.

Limitation of Liability

AgroMath is provided "as is." We are not responsible for delays or failures caused by third-party participants or infrastructure.
